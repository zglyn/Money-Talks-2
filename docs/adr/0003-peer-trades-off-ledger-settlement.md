# Peer Trades with off-ledger Settlement

Teams may agree to move Program Credit between them in exchange for money, but Credit still moves only as a Reallocate under Program Rules and Issuer control. The money leg is Settlement: recorded or demo-simulated, never custodied by CreditPool at the hackathon.

We rejected a public credit exchange and in-app payouts: those imply brokering transferable balances and money-transmitter risk. An Offer → accept → Reallocate flow keeps the issuer-side pitch while making peer intent visible.
